Once an App is installed, you keep full control from your dashboard: you can see its permissions, its installation date, and uninstall it at any time.

## View your installed Apps

From [app.yabetoo.com](https://app.yabetoo.com), click **Apps** in the sidebar. The **Installed** tab lists all Apps currently active on your account.

![List of installed Apps](/images/apps/manage-step-1.png)

Each row displays:

- The App's **name and icon**
- Its **installation date**
- Its **status**: active, suspended, or being uninstalled
- A shortcut to **launch** the App or open its **detail page**

## App detail page

Click an App to open its detail page. You'll find:

![Detail page of an installed App](/images/apps/manage-step-2.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Access key (sk_app_…)" icon="key">
    The access key Yabetoo generated for this App at installation. It's **masked by default** (e.g. `sk_app_****abc1`). You can reveal it temporarily for verification, but it's managed automatically by Yabetoo — you don't need to share it manually.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Granted permissions (scopes)" icon="shield-check">
    The exact list of permissions you approved at installation. This list is **immutable**: if the App needs new permissions, you'll have to explicitly approve them during an update.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Installation date" icon="calendar">
    The exact date and time you installed the App.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Status" icon="circle-info">
    Current operating state: **active** (all good), **suspended** (for safety), or **being uninstalled**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Uninstall an App

You can uninstall an App at any time from its detail page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App's detail page">
    From **Apps > Installed**, click the App you want to uninstall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Uninstall'">
    The button is at the bottom of the detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity">
    For security reasons, Yabetoo requires a **strong confirmation** (password or second factor). This is a sensitive operation: we make sure it's really you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm uninstallation">
    Read the summary of what will happen (tokens revoked, 30-day retention window), then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Uninstall confirmation screen with security step-up](/images/apps/manage-step-3.png)

## What happens after uninstallation?

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Tokens revoked immediately" icon="ban">
    The App instantly loses access to your account. No new API call will be accepted on its behalf.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data kept for 30 days" icon="clock">
    Your business data (menu, orders, configuration) is **stored for 30 days** in the App, read-only.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automatic purge" icon="trash">
    After 30 days, your data is **permanently deleted** in line with our GDPR commitments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  **Changed your mind?** If you reinstall the App **within the 30-day window**, your previous data is automatically reactivated — you pick up where you left off. Beyond that window, the installation starts from a clean slate.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Uninstallation is an **immediate and global** action: all devices, integrations and users tied to this App lose access as soon as you confirm. Let your team know before proceeding.
</Warning>

## Special cases

### App suspended by Yabetoo

In rare cases (security incident, publisher anomaly), Yabetoo may **temporarily suspend** an App. You'll be notified by email and in the dashboard. A suspended App keeps your data but cannot call our APIs until the issue is resolved.

### Deleting your Yabetoo account

If you delete your Yabetoo account, all your Apps are automatically uninstalled in cascade, and the 30-day retention window applies to each one.
