After installation, Takumi walks you through configuring your restaurant. Plan for around **15 to 20 minutes** to go from installation to your first service.

<Note>
  Haven't installed Takumi yet? Start with [Install an App](/en/apps/install-an-app).
</Note>

## Step 1: Create your establishment

On first login at [takumi.yabetoo.com](https://takumi.yabetoo.com), Takumi asks you to create your **Establishment**.

![Creating your first establishment in Takumi](/images/apps/restaurant/setup-establishment.png)

Fill in:

- **Trade name** (e.g. "Brasserie du Plateau")
- **Address** and contact details
- **Timezone** and **currency** (XAF, XOF…)
- **Establishment type** (restaurant, brasserie, snack, food truck)

<Tip>
  If you run multiple venues, start with your main one. You can add more later from settings.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Configure your menu

The menu is the heart of your Takumi. You can build it entirely by hand, or start from a pre-built template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your categories">
    Starters, mains, desserts, drinks… Organise your card into clear sections for your servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your items">
    For each item: name, price, photo (optional), short description. Link it to a category and a kitchen station (hot, cold, bar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define options and extras">
    Cooking, size, sides, paid extras. Everything is configurable per item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Menu configuration with categories and items](/images/apps/restaurant/setup-menu.png)

## Step 3: Draw your floor plan

Takumi lets you model your dining room to make table-side ordering easier.

- Drag and drop your **tables** onto the floor plan
- Set the **number of covers** per table
- Create multiple **zones** (terrace, indoor, bar) if needed

![Interactive floor plan with tables and zones](/images/apps/restaurant/setup-tables.png)

## Step 4: Invite your team

Takumi is multi-user: every team member gets their own access, with a dedicated role.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Server" icon="user">
    Order taking, checkout, table management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cook" icon="kitchen-set">
    KDS access, prep validation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manager" icon="user-tie">
    Full access: menu, team, reports, settings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

From **Settings > Team**, click **Invite a member** and send an email invitation. The member receives a secure link to activate their Takumi account.

<Tip>
  For **kitchen KDS displays**, create a dedicated kitchen account with the Cook role, then sign in on your kitchen screen or tablet. The screen stays signed in throughout the service.
</Tip>

<Card title="Complete KDS guide" icon="book" href="/en/apps/restaurant/kds">
  Connect, configure and supervise a Kitchen Display System (detailed guide — coming soon)
</Card>

## Step 5: Launch your first service

You're all set. You can open your first **shift** (service).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the service">
    From the Takumi home screen, click **Open service**. Takumi starts a new shift and enables order taking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take a first order">
    Select a table, add items from the menu, send to the kitchen. The KDS displays it immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out">
    When the customer asks for the bill, validate the Mobile Money payment — the transaction appears in your Yabetoo Pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the service">
    At the end of the day, click **Close service**. Takumi produces a complete summary (revenue, items sold, performance per server).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your restaurant is now running on Takumi. All transactions checked out flow back automatically to your Yabetoo Pay dashboard.
</Check>
