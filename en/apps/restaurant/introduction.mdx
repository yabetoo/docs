**Takumi by Yabetoo** is the first business App available on the Yabetoo platform. Built for restaurateurs in French-speaking Africa (CEMAC, UEMOA), it brings all the tools of a modern point of sale — directly connected to your Yabetoo Pay account.

<Card title="Launch Takumi" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://takumi.yabetoo.com">
  Open the Restaurant App (requires prior installation from your Yabetoo dashboard)
</Card>

## Who is it for?

Takumi is built for **restaurants, brasseries, snacks and quick-service venues** that want to:

- Take Mobile Money payments without spreadsheets or manual cash registers
- Track kitchen orders in real time
- Centralise menu, stock and team in a single tool
- Keep payments connected to Yabetoo Pay without double entry

<Info>
  Takumi is built for the operational reality of restaurateurs in CEMAC and UEMOA: tablet-based order taking, Mobile Money at the table, occasional offline mode, local currencies (XAF, XOF).
</Info>

## How it works

Takumi connects to your Yabetoo Pay account via the OAuth 2.0 protocol. When you install it from your dashboard, you authorise Takumi to:

- **Issue payments** on your behalf (Mobile Money, cash, other methods)
- **Read your Yabetoo product catalogue** (optional, to prefill the menu)
- **Receive payment confirmations** in real time

All transactions taken via Takumi appear **automatically in your Yabetoo Pay dashboard**, just like any other payment.

## Pricing

<Note>
  Takumi is **included in your standard Yabetoo Pay subscription**: no additional cost to use the App. You only pay the usual transaction fees on Mobile Money payments, according to your Yabetoo pricing.
</Note>

Premium features (multi-venue, advanced integrations, priority support) will be offered in a dedicated tier in Phase 2. You'll be notified beforehand if your usage starts moving into that scope.
