**Yabetoo Apps** are official extensions that add business-specific features to your Yabetoo Pay account. You stay on the same platform, with the same merchant key, while benefiting from specialised tools for your business — a restaurant point-of-sale, accounting synchronisation, and many more to come.

## What is an App?

A Yabetoo App is an application connected to your merchant account via the OAuth 2.0 protocol. It receives strictly the permissions (scopes) it needs, which you explicitly approve before installation, and you can uninstall it at any time from your dashboard.

Concretely, an App lets you, for example:

- Run a restaurant point-of-sale (menu, tables, KDS, service)
- Sync your transactions with an accounting tool
- Issue invoices from your Yabetoo Pay activity
- Connect Yabetoo to your existing stack

<Info>
  All Apps published in Phase 1 are **first-party** — developed and maintained directly by Yabetoo. Third-party Apps will become available in a later phase, with a dedicated review programme.
</Info>

## How to access Apps

All Apps are managed from your Yabetoo Pay dashboard, in the **Apps** section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Dashboard">
    Go to [app.yabetoo.com](https://app.yabetoo.com) and sign in to your merchant account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Apps menu">
    In the sidebar, click **Apps** to access the catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your App">
    Browse available Apps and install the one that fits your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>
