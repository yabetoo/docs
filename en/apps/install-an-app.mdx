Installing a Yabetoo App takes about two minutes. You stay in control of the process: no permission is granted without your explicit approval, and you can uninstall at any time.

## Prerequisites

  <Card title="An active Yabetoo account" icon="user-check">
    Your account must be verified and activated to install business Apps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Administrator rights" icon="shield">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Step 1: Access the App catalogue

Log in to your dashboard at [app.yabetoo.com](https://app.yabetoo.com), then click **Apps** in the sidebar.

![Available Apps catalogue in the dashboard](/images/apps/install-step-1.png)

You'll see all available Apps with their status:

- **Available** — ready to install
- **Installed** — already active on your account
- **Coming soon** — in preparation

## Step 2: Click "Install"

Click the card of the App you want to install your detailed App profile, then click the **Install** button.

![Install button on an App's profile](/images/apps/install-step-2.png)

<Note>
  An App's profile shows its publisher, main features, pricing (if different from your subscription) and the list of requested permissions.
</Note>

## Step 3: Review the requested permissions

Before finalising installation, Yabetoo shows you the **consent screen**: the precise list of data and actions the App will have access to.

![Consent screen listing requested scopes](/images/apps/install-step-3.png)

Each permission (called a *scope*) is explained in plain language. For example:

- **Read payments** — view your account's transaction history
- **Create payments** — issue payment intents on your behalf
- **Read product catalogue** — access your Yabetoo product list

<Warning>
  Read the list of permissions carefully. If a permission seems disproportionate to the App's function, do not install and contact our support at [support@yabetoopay.com](mailto:support@yabetoopay.com).
</Warning>

## Step 4: Approve and get redirected to the App

Click **Authorise installation**. Yabetoo then generates a dedicated access key for this App (in the form `sk_app_…`) and automatically redirects you to the App.

![Automatic redirection to the App after approval](/images/apps/install-step-4.png)

For Takumi (Restaurant App), you land directly on the first establishment setup screen.

<Tip>
  The `sk_app_…` key is managed automatically by Yabetoo. You don't need to **copy and paste anything**: the App authenticates itself through the OAuth 2.0 protocol.
</Tip>

## Step 5: Your App is active

That's it. The App now appears in the **Installed Apps** section of your dashboard, with its installation date, its scopes, and a shortcut to launch it.

<Check>
  Congratulations! Your App is installed and ready to use. You can launch it at any time from your dashboard.
</Check>

## Good to know

<Note>
  **You stay the owner of your data.** The App acts on your behalf, but all the data it generates (payments, customers, products) remains attached to your Yabetoo account. You can uninstall the App at any time — see [Manage your Apps](/en/apps/manage-your-apps).
</Note>
