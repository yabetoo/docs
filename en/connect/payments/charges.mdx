This is the **direct** mode. You name the seller on the payment intent, and Yabetoo splits the
gross amount between them, you and Yabetoo **at the moment the customer pays** — atomically.

<Tip>
Prefer this mode whenever you know the seller at the moment of the sale. It gives the best
traceability: every collection carries the identity of its seller and its commission.
</Tip>

## Create the intent

Add **two fields** to your usual payment intent creation call.

```bash
POST https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com/v1/payment-intents   # Sandbox
POST https://pay.api.yabetoopay.com/v1/payment-intents       # Production
```

| Parameter | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `on_behalf_of` | `string` | The seller's identifier, `acct_…` |
| `application_fee_rate` | `number` | Your commission, **as a percentage** — between `0` and `100` |

<Warning>
**Both go together, or neither.** Supplying only one returns `422 connect.incomplete_request`.
With neither of them, the collection is an ordinary collection on your own account.
</Warning>

<CodeGroup>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com/v1/payment-intents \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 10000,
    "currency": "xaf",
    "on_behalf_of": "acct_01HZVENDOR0000000000000000",
    "application_fee_rate": 10
  }'
```

```javascript fetch
const res = await fetch(
  "https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com/v1/payment-intents",
  {
    method: "POST",
    headers: {
      Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.YABETOO_API_KEY}`,
      "Content-Type": "application/json",
    },
    body: JSON.stringify({
      amount: 10000,
      currency: "xaf",
      on_behalf_of: sellerAccountId,
      application_fee_rate: 10,
    }),
  }
);
```
</CodeGroup>

Confirming the intent is then **identical to an ordinary payment**: see
[Confirm an intent](/en/payments/api/confirm). Nothing changes for the end customer — they pay
as usual.

<Warning>
**Vocabulary collision with Stripe, to be aware of.** At Stripe, `on_behalf_of` designates the
*settlement* merchant and the funds stay with the platform. **At Yabetoo, this field says
where the money GOES.** If you come from Stripe, do not read it backwards.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Why a rate and not an amount.** The gross amount is converted into the currency of the
chosen country at confirmation time: an intent of 100 EUR confirmed in Congo captures about
65,000 XAF, whereas a fixed amount of 20 would stay 20. A rate stays correct whatever the
scale.
</Note>

<Warning>
`destination` is the former name of `on_behalf_of`. It is **rejected with a 422**, never
ignored.
</Warning>

## What happens at capture

On 10,000 XAF, with `application_fee_rate: 10`, in `controller` mode and at the standard
pricing (collection 3.5% + 25, Connect 1%):

| Line | Amount | Destination |
|---|---:|---|
| Gross collected | 10,000 | — |
| Your commission (10% + the 25 fixed fee passed through) | −1,025 | taken from the gross |
| **Seller net** | **8,975** | seller's wallet, as **pending balance** |
| Yabetoo collection | −375 | Yabetoo |
| Yabetoo Connect surcharge | −100 | Yabetoo |
| **Your net** | **550** | your wallet, **available immediately** |

Check: `8 975 + 550 + 475 = 10 000`.

The detail of the two modes and of the refusals: [Commissions and pricing](/en/connect/pricing).

<Warning>
**The seller's net arrives as PENDING balance**, not as available balance. It becomes
withdrawable after the availability delay. See [Balances and payouts](/en/connect/payouts).
</Warning>

## Checks made at creation

Yabetoo refuses **when the intent is created**, not when the customer pays. This is
deliberate: a contract refusal must reach your developer, not your buyer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The seller really is yours">
    Otherwise `403`. An unknown seller and a seller of another marketplace return the same
    response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The commission mode is known">
    Otherwise `422 connect.fee_payer_unset` — activate Connect on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your commission covers the Yabetoo fees">
    In `controller` mode only. Otherwise `400 connect.application_fee_too_low`, with the rate
    floor and the required minimum in the body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The seller would receive a positive amount">
    Otherwise `422 connect.vendor_net_not_positive`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Errors

| Status | Code | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `connect.application_fee_too_low` | Your commission does not exceed the Yabetoo fees |
| `400` | `connect.application_fee_too_high` | Your commission exceeds the amount collected |
| `401` | — | Test key targeting a `live` seller |
| `403` | — | Unknown, malformed, or another marketplace's seller |
| `422` | `connect.incomplete_request` | `on_behalf_of` and `application_fee_rate` not supplied together |
| `422` | `connect.fee_payer_unset` | Connect is not activated on your account |
| `422` | `connect.vendor_net_not_positive` | The seller would receive nothing |
| `422` | `rule: "unsupported"` | `destination` sent instead of `on_behalf_of` |
| `503` | `E_CONNECT_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE` | The commission grid could not be resolved. Connect **refuses** rather than charging zero. |

## The alternative

If you do not yet know the seller at the moment of the sale — multi-seller cart, split
computed after the fact — collect normally on your account and split afterwards:
[Deferred mode](/en/connect/payments/allocations).
