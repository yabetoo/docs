description: "Get your seller's identity verified and their payout destination collected."

A seller who has just been created **can receive nothing**. They must first open their
verification file and submit their documents and their payout destination.

Yabetoo hosts this flow. You generate a link, you pass it to the seller, they complete it.

## Generate an onboarding link

```bash
POST https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com/v1/connect/accounts/{acct}/onboarding_links   # Sandbox
POST https://pay.api.yabetoopay.com/v1/connect/accounts/{acct}/onboarding_links       # Production
```

<CodeGroup>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST \
  https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com/v1/connect/accounts/acct_01HZVENDOR0000000000000000/onboarding_links \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY"
```
</CodeGroup>

The request body is **empty**.

```json 201
{
  "object": "connect_onboarding_link",
  "url": "https://verify.yabetoo.com/flow?token=vsess_...",
  "expires_at": "2026-09-06T10:00:00.000Z"
}
```

<Note>
**No `Idempotency-Key` on this route**, unlike seller creation. The call **resumes** an
ongoing session instead of opening a second one: you can replay it as many times as needed to
regenerate the link of a seller who has lost theirs.
</Note>

### Rejected fields

| Field | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `country` | `422`, `rule: "derived"` | The compliance requirements are derived from the country of the **connected account**, set at its creation. |
| `kycLevel` | `422`, `rule: "derived"` | The level is derived from `country × subject type`. You do not choose the compliance regime of your seller. |

## What the seller does there

<Steps>
  <Step title="They open the link">
    The page is hosted by Yabetoo (`verify.yabetoo.com`). You can open it in a tab, send it
    by email, or embed it in an iframe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They submit their documents">
    Identity document and selfie for level 1. Proof of address or trade register for level 2,
    which raises the limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They register their payout destination">
    This is **their payout destination**. It is the seller who submits it, not you — and that
    is why `phone` is rejected when the account is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yabetoo reviews the file">
    Automatic verification when it is possible, human review otherwise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**There is no `return_url`.** Connect v1 exposes no redirection parameter: the seller finishes
on a Yabetoo page. If you load the link in an iframe, the end of the flow is notified to you
via `postMessage`. Otherwise, track progress through the
[webhooks](/en/connect/webhooks) or by querying the account.
</Warning>

## During verification

| | Allowed |
|---|---|
| The seller **collects** | ✅ Yes — their funds accumulate as pending balance |
| The seller **withdraws** | ❌ No — nothing can leave before approval |

This is deliberate: your seller can sell from day one, but the money does not leave the system
until their identity is established.

<Warning>
**A consequence to know about.** A seller can accumulate funds and then have their file
**rejected**. Their funds are then refunded to the buyers, charge by charge. Do not promise an
unverified seller that their balance is theirs to keep.
</Warning>

## Track progress

Query the account to read its `status` and its balances:

```bash
GET /v1/connect/accounts/{acct}
```

See [Manage your sellers](/en/connect/accounts/manage).

## Errors

| Status | Code | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `401` | — | You are targeting a `live` seller with a test key (or the other way round) |
| `403` | — | Unknown seller, malformed identifier, or seller of another marketplace — **the response is identical in all three cases** |
| `422` | `rule: "derived"` | `country` or `kycLevel` sent |
| `502` | `E_CONNECT_ONBOARDING_UNSUPPORTED` | No compliance requirements are configured for this seller's country. Contact support — retrying will change nothing. |
| `502` | `E_REFERENTIAL_COUNTRY_MISSING` | The account's country cannot be found in the reference data |
| `503` | `E_SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | The verification service is unavailable — retry |

<Note>
The `403` codes never distinguish "this seller does not exist" from "this seller is not
yours". This is intentional: otherwise the API would become a way to test the existence of
other people's accounts.
</Note>
