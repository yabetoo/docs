Connect is tested with your `sk_test_` keys, on the same endpoints as in production.

## The environment is inherited, never chosen

<Warning>
**An `sk_test_` key only creates test sellers, and can only target them.** A connected
account's environment is inherited from the key that created it — there is no field to choose
it.

Targeting a `live` seller with a test key — or the reverse — returns `401`, on **every**
Connect route. This is not a key bug: it is the isolation barrier.
</Warning>

This is the first symptom to recognise: a `401` on a seller you have just created almost always
means you switched keys between the two calls.

## 1. Activate Connect on your test account

```bash
curl -X POST https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com/v1/connect/activations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "connect_mode": "marketplace" }'
```

<Warning>
**The mode is immutable, in test too.** You will not be able to flip your test account from
`marketplace` to `platform` to compare the two cascades. To test both modes, you need **two
separate merchant accounts**.
</Warning>

## 2. Create test sellers

Nothing sets test seller creation apart: same fields, same mandatory `Idempotency-Key`.

<Tip>
Create **at least two**, and keep one you never verify: it is the only way to exercise the
verification-related refusals (`403 E_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`,
`422 E_CONNECT_VENDOR_PAYOUT_METHOD_UNAVAILABLE`).
</Tip>

<Tip>
Also create a seller under a **second merchant account**. It is the only way to check that your
code handles correctly the `403` of a seller that does not belong to you — a case you will hit
in production as soon as an identifier slips from one tenant to another.
</Tip>

## 3. Verification is not instant in test

<Warning>
**Do not rely on automatic approval in test.** You will get the link, and the seller can
submit their documents and Mobile Money number, but the file goes through a **review**: moving
to `approved` is neither immediate nor guaranteed.

To obtain an **approved** test seller — required to exercise withdrawals — contact
[support@yabetoopay.com](mailto:support@yabetoopay.com).
</Warning>

What you can verify without approval:

- link generation and expiry (`expires_at`);
- the fact that regenerating the link **resumes** the session instead of opening a second one;
- the `422` refusals on `country` and `kycLevel`;
- that funds do accumulate while no withdrawal is possible.

## 4. Test the collection and the cascade

The split is computed at **capture** time. Use the usual Mobile Money test numbers to drive the
payment outcome — see
[Test your integration](/en/developer-tools/test/overview).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the intent with the Connect fields">
    ```json
    { "amount": 10000, "currency": "xaf",
      "on_behalf_of": "acct_...", "application_fee_rate": 10 }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a test number that succeeds">
    The split only happens on a successful capture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read back the seller's balances">
    ```bash
    GET /v1/connect/accounts/{acct}
    ```
    You must see the seller's net in `pending_balance`, and **`balance` at zero**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Check conservation, not just the HTTP code.** On every capture,
`seller net + your net + Yabetoo fees` must equal the gross exactly. That is the assertion that
catches a mode or a unit error; a `201` proves nothing.
</Tip>

### Exercising the commission refusals

| To obtain | Send |
|---|---|
| `400 connect.application_fee_too_low` | an `application_fee_rate` **at or below** your grid's floor (4.5% on the standard rate card) |
| `400 connect.application_fee_too_high` | a rate that produces a commission greater than the gross |
| `422 connect.incomplete_request` | `on_behalf_of` without `application_fee_rate` |
| `422 connect.fee_payer_unset` | a Connect call **before** activating Connect |
| `403` | a made-up, malformed `acct_`, or one belonging to another merchant |

<Note>
The `too_low` refusal does **not** depend on the amount: a rate below the floor fails at 100 XAF
just as at 1,000,000. There is no point looking for an amount that triggers it.
</Note>

## 5. The availability delay

<Warning>
**This is the main difficulty of end-to-end testing.** A seller's funds land in
`pending_balance` and only become withdrawable after the availability delay — **7 days by
default**. There is no test clock and no endpoint to force maturation.

To test the full chain in a single session, ask support for a shortened delay on your test
environment.
</Warning>

In the meantime, you can verify:

- that `next_maturity_at` carries the expected date;
- that `available_at` is set and `matured_at` null on the transaction;
- that a withdrawal against a still-pending balance does return
  `422 E_CONNECT_VENDOR_EMPTY_BALANCE` — and not a generic error.

## 6. Test deferred mode without waiting

[Deferred mode](/en/connect/payments/allocations) is exercised **without a real collection**:
all you need is a balance on your own merchant wallet.

It is the fastest way to verify:

- the 1% surcharge and the meaning of `amount` in the response;
- a partial then a full reversal, and the `reversed_total` running total;
- the `422 connect.reversal_exceeds_remaining` when you exceed the remainder;
- the `402` on a reversal when the seller cannot return the funds.

## 7. Test idempotency for real

<Tip>
Replay every money call **with the same `Idempotency-Key`** and check that no second operation
was created — by reading back the balances, not by reading the HTTP code.

Check the reverse case too: the **same key on two different sellers** must produce **two**
operations. An implementation that deduplicates there would only pay one seller out of two.
</Tip>

## What cannot be tested

| | Why |
|---|---|
| `platform` mode on an account activated in `marketplace` | `connect_mode` is immutable |
| Unlinking or transferring a seller | These operations do not exist |
| Multi-currency | A different currency is refused |
| Automatic KYC approval | Review is not automatic in test |
| Automatic return at the end of onboarding | Connect v1 exposes no `return_url` |

## Going live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate Connect with your live key">
    Activation is **per account and per environment**: activating in test does not activate in
    production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recreate your sellers">
    A test seller does not exist in production. No migration is possible — and their
    verification has to be done again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rewire your webhooks">
    Check that you subscribe to the **bare** names (`connect.transfer.created`), and that your
    routing does not assume `accountId` is your own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check how you handle the 402">
    It is the refusal you will probably never see in test, and the one that costs the most in
    production. Subscribe to `connect.refund.blocked_insufficient_funds`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
