Yabetoo Connect lets a **marketplace** collect payments on behalf of **sellers**
it has connected, take its commission along the way, and pay the sellers out to their payout destination.

You keep a single integration, a single API key and a single cash flow. Yabetoo maintains the
ledger for each seller.

<Info>
**Environments.** Connect runs on the same hosts as the rest of the Yabetoo API:
`https://pay.sandbox.yabetoopay.com` with an `sk_test_` key, and
`https://pay.api.yabetoopay.com` with an `sk_live_` key. Both serve the same endpoints.
</Info>

## The three parties

Connect brings three parties together. The vocabulary is fixed throughout the documentation
and the API — confusing it is the number one source of integration errors.

| Party | Who | In the API |
|---|---|---|
| **Yabetoo** | the payment provider | — |
| **The marketplace** | you, Yabetoo's customer | the *controller* — your `sk_` key signs the requests |
| **The seller** | the third party you connect | the *connected account*, identified by an `acct_…` |

## How the money flows

The money **always** lands on Yabetoo's operator identity, then Connect splits it in the
ledger. There is no routing to a third-party operator account: the split is a book entry, so
it is atomic.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You create a seller">
    One API call creates their account, their organization and their wallet.
    See [Create a connected account](/en/connect/accounts/create).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The seller completes verification">
    You send them an onboarding link hosted by Yabetoo. There they submit their identity
    documents and their payout destination.
    See [Onboarding and verification](/en/connect/accounts/onboarding).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You collect">
    Either you name the seller at capture time and the split happens immediately
    ([direct mode](/en/connect/payments/charges)), or you collect into your own account and
    distribute later ([deferred mode](/en/connect/payments/allocations)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The funds mature">
    Every seller credit arrives as a **pending balance** and becomes available after a
    configurable delay. See [Balances and availability](/en/connect/payouts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You pay the seller">
    You order the withdrawal of their available balance to their payout destination, on
    demand or on an automatic schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The two commission modes

Connect supports two economic configurations, chosen **once and for all** when you activate
your account. This is the `fee_payer` setting, and it decides **who bears the Yabetoo fees**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Marketplace mode" icon="store" href="/en/connect/pricing">
    `fee_payer = controller`

    You take a commission on the sale, and the Yabetoo fees come out of **your commission**.
    The seller sees a single deduction: yours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Platform mode" icon="layer-group" href="/en/connect/pricing">
    `fee_payer = account`

    The seller is billed by Yabetoo **like an ordinary merchant**, and your commission is
    added on top. You keep your commission in full.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
The same rate does not earn the same thing in both modes. A controller declaring 5% keeps
**50** on a 10,000 XAF sale in marketplace mode, and **500** in platform mode. Read
[Commissions and pricing](/en/connect/pricing) before you choose.
</Warning>

## What Connect does — and does not do

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A seller belongs to exactly one marketplace">
    The control link is set at creation and it is **immutable**. There is no endpoint to
    unlink a seller, nor to transfer them to another marketplace. Multi-attachment is not
    supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A seller cannot itself control other sellers">
    Control depth is limited to 1. A connected account cannot become a marketplace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No wallet can go negative">
    A seller's wallet cannot be debited after the fact. Yabetoo therefore never
    advances funds: a refund that is not covered by the available balances is **refused**, it
    does not create a debt. See [Refunds](/en/connect/refunds).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One currency per relationship">
    A seller is created in their marketplace's currency. Multi-currency is not supported in
    v1: a different currency is refused at creation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The seller has no dashboard">
    By default a connected account has no login, no API key and no dashboard. They submit
    their documents through the onboarding link, and you are the one who orders their
    withdrawals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
