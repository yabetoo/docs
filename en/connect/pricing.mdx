On every Connect collection, the gross amount is split between **three** parties: the seller,
you, and Yabetoo. This page describes exactly what each one receives, and why some commissions
are refused.

## The Yabetoo rate card

Two lines apply to a Connect collection. They are distinct and they add up.

| Line | What it charges | Standard rate card (Congo) |
|---|---|---|
| `collection` | the collection itself — it exists for every merchant | **3.5% + 25 XAF** |
| `connect` | the Connect relationship: seller verification, onboarding, payout rails | **1%** |

On 10,000 XAF of gross, Yabetoo therefore collects `375 + 100 = 475 XAF`.

<Note>
This rate card is **negotiable per organization**. Never hard-code 4.5% in your code: read your
actual grid with [`GET /v1/commissions/types`](/en/api-reference/introduction) and
`POST /v1/commissions/calculate`.
</Note>

<Warning>
**The fixed fee makes the effective rate depend on the amount.** The 25 XAF do not dilute:

| Gross | Yabetoo deduction | As % of gross |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 500 | 47.50 | **9.5%** |
| 1,000 | 70.00 | **7.0%** |
| 10,000 | 475.00 | 4.75% |
| 100,000 | 4,525.00 | 4.53% |

This is what **the seller** bears. It does not, however, change the refusal described below,
which is a comparison of **rates**.
</Warning>

## Declaring your commission

You declare your commission **on every collection**, on the payment intent, as a **rate**.

```json
{
  "amount": 10000,
  "currency": "xaf",
  "on_behalf_of": "acct_01HZVENDOR000000000000000",
  "application_fee_rate": 5
}
```

| Field | Type | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `on_behalf_of` | `string` | the seller's identifier, `acct_…` |
| `application_fee_rate` | `number` | **percentage**, between `0` and `100` |

<Warning>
**Both fields go together or neither.** Providing only one returns
`422 connect.incomplete_request`.
</Warning>

<Warning>
`application_fee_rate` is expressed as a **percentage**: `5` means 5%. `0.05` would mean 0.05%.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Why a rate and not an amount.** The gross is converted into the currency of the chosen
country at confirmation time: an intent of 100 EUR confirmed in Congo captures roughly
65,000 XAF, whereas a fixed amount of 20 would stay 20. A rate, by contrast, applies to the
actual gross.

If you need a flat amount, compute it on your side and express it as a rate for the sale
concerned.
</Note>

<Note>
**`fee_payer` is not passed in.** It is read from the control relationship, as you set it at
[activation](/en/connect/activate). Posting it is refused.
</Note>

### The rate form passes on our fixed fee (marketplace mode only)

In `controller` mode, a rate of 5% does not charge the seller 5%: it charges
**5% + our fixed share**. You declare a single number — the fixed part is ours, passed on
automatically, so that your margin does not depend on the basket.

```
commission charged = your_rate × gross + collection_fixed_fee + connect_fixed_fee
```

| | 100 XAF sale | 10,000 XAF sale |
|---|---:|---:|
| commission charged to the seller (5% + 25) | **30.00** | **525.00** |
| ├ Yabetoo collection (3.5% + 25) | 28.50 | 375.00 |
| ├ Connect surcharge (1%) | 1.00 | 100.00 |
| └ **your margin** | **0.50** | **50.00** |
| the seller receives | **70.00** | **9,475.00** |

Your margin is exactly `0.5% × gross` in both cases: it no longer depends on the basket.

<Warning>
**Tell your sellers.** On a 100 XAF sale, the seller receives **70**, not 95: the 25 XAF fixed
fee is inside what you charge them. Advertise "5% + 25 XAF", not "5%".
</Warning>

## The two modes — `fee_payer`

The mode decides **who bears the Yabetoo fees**. It moves the charge, it never removes it: the
Connect surcharge applies in both modes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Marketplace mode (controller)">
    The Yabetoo fees are taken **out of your commission**. The seller sees a single deduction:
    yours.

    ```
    seller     = gross − commission
    you        = commission − (collection + connect)
    Yabetoo    = collection + connect
    ```

    Your margin is the **residue**. That is why there is a floor, and why our fixed share is
    passed on: your commission **funds** our fees.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Platform mode (account)">
    The seller is billed by Yabetoo **like an ordinary merchant**, and your commission is added
    on top. You keep your commission in full.

    ```
    seller     = gross − commission − (collection + connect)
    you        = commission
    Yabetoo    = collection + connect
    ```

    Nothing to fund, therefore **no floor**: your commission can be zero. And **no passing on
    of the fixed fee** — adding it would make the seller pay it twice.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
**The same rate does not earn the same thing.** A controller declaring 5% on a 10,000 XAF sale
keeps **50** in marketplace mode (the residue above Yabetoo's 475) and **500** in platform mode
(their entire margin). A factor of ten. And the seller receives 9,475 in the first case against
9,025 in the second.
</Warning>

### Comparison table

On 10,000 XAF of gross, standard rate card, with `application_fee_rate`:

| | marketplace | platform | platform + commission |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `fee_payer` of the relationship | `controller` | `account` | `account` |
| `application_fee_rate` | `10` | `0` | `10` |
| commission actually taken | 1,025 | 0 | 1,000 |
| **the seller receives** | **8,975** | **9,525** | **8,525** |
| **you receive** | **550** | **0** | **1,000** |
| **Yabetoo collects** | **475** | **475** | **475** |

In all three cases the sum is exactly 10,000: Connect neither creates nor destroys money.

<Note>
"Commission actually taken" line: in `controller` mode, the 25 XAF fixed fee is **added** to
your rate (1,000 + 25); in `account` mode, it is not — the seller already pays it separately,
and passing it on would make them pay it twice.
</Note>

## Refusals, and how to avoid them

### `connect.application_fee_too_low` — 400

<ResponseField name="Mode concerned" type="controller only">
  Your commission must **exceed** — not merely cover — the Yabetoo fees. Equality is refused:
  it would leave you at zero net, which is not a viable operation.
</ResponseField>

```json
{
  "status": 400,
  "code": "connect.application_fee_too_low",
  "message": "...",
  "minimum_rate_excluded": 4.5,
  "requested_rate": 4,
  "minimum": 475,
  "requested": 425,
  "currency": "xaf"
}
```

<ResponseField name="minimum_rate_excluded" type="number">
  The **rate floor**, as a percentage. On the standard rate card it is **4.5%** — the
  collection rate (3.5%) plus the Connect rate (1%).

  It is called *excluded* because the bound is **strict**: at exactly 4.5%, it is still
  refused.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="requested_rate" type="number">
  The rate you sent.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
**This refusal does not depend on the amount.** Because our fixed component is passed through
inside your commission, it appears on both sides of the comparison and cancels out. The guard
reduces to:

```
your_rate > collection_rate + connect_rate
```

A rate above the floor passes at **every** amount. A rate equal to or below it is refused at
**every** amount — you will not see "some" captures fail, you will see all of them fail.

The fix is therefore to revisit your pricing, never the sale in progress.
</Warning>

<Note>
In `account` mode there is **no floor**: your commission has nothing to fund, so it may be
zero. This refusal never occurs there.
</Note>

<Tip>
**The simple rule:** declare a rate strictly above your rate card's floor and you will never be
refused for this reason — whatever the amount of the sale.
</Tip>

### `connect.application_fee_too_high` — 400

Your commission exceeds the amount collected.

```json
{
  "status": 400,
  "code": "connect.application_fee_too_high",
  "requested": 12000,
  "gross_amount": 10000,
  "currency": "xaf"
}
```

### `connect.vendor_net_not_positive` — 422

After all deductions, the seller would receive zero or less. There is nothing to credit.

### `connect.incomplete_request` — 422

`on_behalf_of` and `application_fee_rate` must be provided **together**.

### `connect.fee_payer_unset` — 422

The connected account targeted has no usable `fee_payer` — activate Connect on your account
before collecting. See [Activate Connect](/en/connect/activate).

### `E_CONNECT_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE` — 503

The commission grid could not be resolved. Connect **fails closed**: it never charges a
fallback rate. Retry; if the refusal persists, contact support — a rate card line is missing
for your organization.

## Rounding

Amounts are computed in exact decimal then rounded to two decimal places (`HALF_UP` for the
fees, `ROUND_DOWN` for the captured amount). The rounding residue goes to **Yabetoo**: the
seller and you receive deterministic amounts.

<Note>
XAF has no subunit. The amounts that actually leave the system towards the operator are whole numbers; the fractions stay internal to the ledger.
</Note>

## What about deferred mode?

An [allocation](/en/connect/payments/allocations) also carries the 1% Connect surcharge,
calculated on the **allocated amount** — not on the original gross. Without that, "collect
without a seller then allocate" would reach the same economic outcome while paying 1% less.

The relationship's `fee_payer` applies there in the same way: in `controller` mode you are
debited `amount + 1%` and the seller receives `amount`; in `account` mode the seller receives
`amount − 1%`.
